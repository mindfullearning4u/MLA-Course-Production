# Moodle Course Builder

# Part 1 — Core Instructional Pages

---

# Purpose

This section defines the required construction standards for every core instructional page within an MLA Moodle lesson.

These pages form the instructional foundation of every lesson.

Regardless of subject, every lesson begins with these instructional components before students proceed to guided instruction, practice, checkpoints, or assessments.

The Course Architecture and Planning Agent determines:

- whether a page is required,
- the instructional purpose,
- the lesson sequence,
- the Moodle activity type,
- the required resources,
- the required evidence.

The Moodle Course Builder Agent constructs the page exactly as specified.

No instructional decisions should be made during construction.

---

# Standard Construction Rule

Every page built inside Moodle must contain:

1. Purpose
2. Moodle Activity Type
3. Placement
4. Required Components
5. Student Directions
6. Student Focus
7. Required Evidence
8. Visual Requirements
9. Accessibility Requirements
10. Moodle Configuration
11. Definition of Done

No page type is exempt.

---

# Lesson Overview Page

## Purpose

The Lesson Overview prepares students for the lesson.

Students should immediately understand:

• what they will learn

• why the lesson matters

• what work they will complete

• what evidence they must submit

• how the lesson connects to previous learning

• how the lesson prepares them for future lessons

This page is the student's roadmap.

---

## Moodle Activity Type

Page

Do not build as:

Assignment

Quiz

Book

Forum

URL

---

## Placement

Always the first page of every lesson.

Nothing instructional appears before this page.

---

## Required Header

Every Lesson Overview begins with:

Course Name

Unit Number

Lesson Number

Lesson Title

MLA Lesson ID

Example

BIOLOGY

Unit 1

Lesson 2

Scientific Investigation

BIO-U1-L2

The header must match the approved MLA format.

---

## Lesson Focus

One concise paragraph.

Explain:

What students will learn.

Why it is important.

How it connects to previous lessons.

How it prepares them for later lessons.

Avoid generic wording.

---

## Learning Objectives

Objectives must be measurable.

Use verbs such as:

Explain

Analyze

Interpret

Compare

Calculate

Construct

Evaluate

Design

Identify

Differentiate

Avoid

Understand

Know

Learn about

Become familiar with

Objectives should describe observable performance.

---

## Standards

Display only the primary MLA standards.

Do not display large tables of Florida or Common Core codes.

Those belong in the Crosswalk.

Students only need the instructional focus.

---

## Evidence Checklist

Students should immediately know:

What work must be retained.

What work must be submitted.

Separate evidence into categories.

Example

Notebook Evidence

Vocabulary

Notebook Task 1

Notebook Task 2

Lesson Review

Independent Application

Checkpoint Evidence

Investigation

Data Table

Written Analysis

Assessment

Lesson Quiz

Students should know every required product before beginning the lesson.

---

## Student Expectations

State clearly:

Complete every required page.

Complete activities in sequence.

Return to Moodle after Savvas.

Maintain your notebook.

Submit required evidence.

Review Teacher of Record feedback.

Students should understand expectations before beginning.

---

## Help and TOR Support

Every Lesson Overview ends with:

Need Help?

Contact your Teacher of Record if:

A Moodle link fails.

A Savvas resource is unavailable.

Instructions remain unclear.

Include:

Course

Unit

Lesson

Page Title

Screenshot when appropriate.

---

## Visual Requirements

Include:

Approved Course Header

Learning Objectives Box

Evidence Checklist Box

Help and TOR Support Box

Approved Color Scheme

Approved Typography

Consistent Spacing

Students should immediately recognize the Lesson Overview page.

---

## Accessibility

Verify:

Heading hierarchy

Readable spacing

Alternative text

Descriptive headings

Color contrast

Keyboard accessibility when applicable

---

## Moodle Configuration

Activity Type

Page

Completion

Students must view page.

Restrictions

None unless approved.

---

## Definition of Done

Lesson Overview is complete when:

✓ Header exists

✓ Objectives exist

✓ Evidence Checklist exists

✓ Student Expectations exist

✓ TOR Support exists

✓ Accessibility verified

✓ Moodle settings verified

# Vocabulary Page

## Purpose

Introduce the academic language required for the lesson.

Vocabulary should be presented before students encounter the terminology in instruction.

---

## Moodle Activity Type

Page

---

## Placement

Immediately before students begin reading or instruction.

---

## Required Components

Vocabulary Table

Term

Definition

Student-friendly explanation

Optional pronunciation

Optional image

Vocabulary must match approved Savvas terminology unless otherwise approved.

---

## Student Directions

Students should:

Read every term.

Review every definition.

Write every term in their notebook.

Write the definition in their own words when instructed.

Retain notebook work.

---

## Student Focus

Explain why the vocabulary matters.

Example

These terms will be used throughout the lesson review, checkpoint, and unit assessment.

---

## Required Evidence

Vocabulary notebook entries.

Required diagrams when applicable.

Definitions.

---

## Visual Requirements

Vocabulary Box

Readable table

Clear spacing

Consistent formatting

---

## Accessibility

Table headings.

Alternative text.

Readable fonts.

---

## Moodle Configuration

Activity Type

Page

Completion

Student views page.

---

## Definition of Done

Vocabulary page complete.

Students know:

Every required term.

Every required definition.

Why the vocabulary matters.

# Notebook Task Page

## Purpose

Notebook Tasks require students to actively process lesson content.

Notebook Tasks become permanent learning evidence.

Notebook Tasks are required.

---

## Moodle Activity Type

Page

Notebook submission is created separately.

---

## Placement

Exactly where specified in the Unit and Lesson Map.

---

## Required Components

Notebook Task Title

Purpose

Student Directions

Notebook Requirements

Evidence Reminder

Submission Reminder

---

## Student Directions

Specify exactly:

What students write.

How responses are organized.

Number responses.

Complete sentence requirements.

Required calculations.

Required graphs.

Required diagrams.

Required labels.

Never write:

Complete this in your notebook.

Always explain exactly what students must produce.

---

## Notebook Standards

Every notebook page should contain:

Student Name

Course

Unit

Lesson

MLA Lesson ID

Notebook Task Title

Students should always know how to organize notebook work.

---

## Required Evidence

Specify:

Vocabulary

Written responses

Calculations

Tables

Graphs

Diagrams

Analysis

Evidence required for grading.

---

## Submission Reminder

Students complete the notebook here.

Students submit later.

Do not instruct students to upload notebook work from this page unless the Lesson Blueprint specifically requires it.

---

## Visual Requirements

Notebook Task Box

Instruction Box

Evidence Reminder Box

---

## Accessibility

Readable spacing.

Descriptive headings.

Consistent formatting.

---

## Moodle Configuration

Activity Type

Page

Completion

Student views page.

---

## Definition of Done

Notebook Task complete.

Students know:

Exactly what to write.

Exactly what evidence must be retained.

Exactly where submission occurs.

# Savvas Lesson Page

## Purpose

Direct students into the approved Savvas instructional resource.

Students should never wonder:

Where do I begin?

Where do I stop?

---

## Moodle Activity Type

URL

or

Page containing approved resource links.

---

## Required Components

Resource Title

Purpose

Student Directions

Student Focus

Notebook Reminder

Completion Reminder

---

## Resource Information

Display:

Exact Savvas Title

Exact Lesson

Exact Topic

Exact Resource

Do not abbreviate.

---

## Student Directions

Always specify:

Click

Open

Begin at

Complete

Stop after

Return to Moodle

Never write:

Complete the Savvas lesson.

Students must know exactly where instruction begins and ends.

---

## Student Focus

Explain what students should notice.

Examples

Observe

Identify

Compare

Analyze

Record

Students should understand why they are using the resource.

---

## Notebook Reminder

Specify:

Exactly what should be written.

Exactly where.

Exactly how.

---

## Required Evidence

Identify:

Notebook responses

Tables

Graphs

Written analysis

Calculations

Vocabulary

---

## Visual Requirements

Resource Box

Navigation Button

Notebook Reminder

---

## Accessibility

Descriptive button text.

Alternative text.

Readable spacing.

---

## Moodle Configuration

Activity Type

URL

Completion

Student opens resource.

---

## Definition of Done

Students know:

Where to begin.

Where to stop.

What to record.

What to do after Savvas.
